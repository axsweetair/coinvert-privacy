LootX is a crypto market-analytics app and a client interface to your own cryptocurrency exchange account. Contact: lootx.ceo@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a username / account ID, collected when you register and sign in, used to create and secure your account.
  • Trade journal. If you use the trade-journal feature, the trades you execute are sent to and stored on our server to build your personal trade history and statistics.
  • Content you create. Chart drawings and team alerts / team membership you create are stored on our servers so they sync across your devices and, for teams, are shared with your team.

All of the above is used only to provide app functionality. We do not use it for tracking or advertising, and we do not sell it.

Information that stays on your device

  • Exchange API keys. If you choose to connect your own exchange account, your API keys are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted to or stored on our servers. They are used solely to display your balance and positions and to send order requests directly to your own exchange.
  • App preferences and settings are stored on your device.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect or store your exchange API keys.
  • We do not hold, custody, or transfer your funds.
  • We do not track you or sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

Market data

Prices, order books, open interest and funding are retrieved from public third-party exchange APIs (e.g. Binance, Bybit, OKX, CoinGecko). No personal information is sent to these providers.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information and synced content while your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time at lootx.ceo@gmail.com.

Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect your information. Account credentials are stored in the device Keychain, and exchange API keys never leave your device.

Children

LootX is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly.

Какие данные мы собираем

  • Данные аккаунта. Ваш email и имя пользователя / идентификатор аккаунта — собираются при регистрации и входе, используются для создания и защиты аккаунта.

Чего мы НЕ делаем

  • Мы не собираем и не храним ваши API-ключи биржи.
  • Мы не храним и не переводим ваши средства.
  • Мы не отслеживаем вас и не продаём ваши персональные данные.
  • Мы не используем ваши данные для рекламы.

Рыночные данные

Цены, стаканы, открытый интерес и funding загружаются из публичных API сторонних бирж (например, Binance, Bybit, OKX, CoinGecko). Персональные данные им не передаются.

Мы храним данные аккаунта и синхронизируемый контент, пока существует ваш аккаунт. Вы можете в любой момент запросить удаление аккаунта и связанных данных по адресу lootx.ceo@gmail.com.
