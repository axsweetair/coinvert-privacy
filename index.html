CoinVert Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

CoinVert ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information.

Information We Collect

CoinVert does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app works entirely on your device.

Data Storage

All user preferences (selected currencies, language choice) are stored locally on your device only. No data is sent to our servers. We do not have servers that store user data.

Third-Party Services

The app fetches real-time exchange rates from the following public APIs:

  • ExchangeRate API (open.er-api.com) — for fiat currency rates
  • CoinGecko API (api.coingecko.com) — for cryptocurrency rates

These services may collect anonymous usage data (such as IP address) according to their own privacy policies. We do not share any user data with these services beyond what is required for the API request.

Analytics & Advertising

CoinVert does not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your usage in any way.

Children's Privacy

CoinVert does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app does not require registration and does not collect any personal data from any user.

Data Security

All network requests to exchange rate APIs are made over HTTPS (encrypted connections). Since we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no risk of data breach from our side.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete. You can uninstall the app at any time to remove all locally stored preferences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
